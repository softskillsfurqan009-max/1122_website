Biography

About Rehana Yasmeen

Editable Content [Add Rehana Yasmeen's personal story, education and mission statement here.]

Rehana Yasmeen serves as an Emergency Officer at the Sindh Emergency Rescue Service 1122 — Pakistan's premier emergency response force. On the frontlines of emergency care, she has witnessed firsthand how the first few minutes of a crisis determine its outcome.

As a certified American Heart Association instructor, she trains individuals, schools, workplaces and communities in CPR and first aid — turning ordinary citizens into confident first responders.

Through media appearances on Masala TV and public awareness campaigns, she continues to spread one simple message: every bystander can save a life.
